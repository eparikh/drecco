<p>Two or more players are presented with some number of stones. The winner is the player who removes the last stone.
</p>

<p> <strong> Rules: </strong>
</p><ul>
<li>By default each player is dealt a set of cards with numbers 1 through # or cards unless the players click the "Use random card" box. If they do click that box, each player will be dealt a set of cards chosen randomly and perhaps with repetition between 1 and # of cards.</li>
<li>The first player chooses a card and removes exactly that number of stones. The card then disappears from the first player's hand. Similarly for the 2nd, 3rd ... player.</li>

<li>Play continues until on his or her turn, a player either:</li>
<ul>
<li>chooses a card whose number exactly matches the number of stones remaining in which case that player wins.
</li>
<li>chooses a card that exceeds the number of stones remaining in which case that player loses
</li>
</ul>

<p>If all the player's cards exceed the number of stones remaining, the player loses automatically</p>

<p>If the player exhausts the time allowance, the player loses automatically.</p>            </ul>
