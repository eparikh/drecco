<div>
<p> You have a certain number (default = 3) of covered boxes of Burmese rubies before you. The total number of rubies is by default 29 (and must be odd).  </p>
<p> Each of the two players is allowed to ask for a certain number of rubies from each box. There are two possible cases: </p>
<p>
</p><li> In a turn, a player selects a box and enters an amount. If the player asks for more than there are in the box, they get none from it </li>

<li> Otherwise, the player gets what the player  asked for from that box </li>


When all rubies have been taken the winner is the one with the most rubies. <p></p>

<strong> Instructions </strong>

<li> Press pop-up to access game window. </li>

<li> ENTERING PHASE: When it is your turn, type your amount in the box. Then click "submit move" to end your turn. </li>

<li> DISPLAY PHASE: After all the amounts are collected you will see how many rubies you obtained. </li>
<p></p>
<p> <strong> Note: </strong> For best experience, maximize window as much as possible. </p>
