## Rules

Claim more lands than your opponent before running out of walls!

In each round, you can start building your wall at any land adjacent to your territory. Lands surrounded by your wall will (temporarily) become your territory. Your wall cannot intersect with your land or itself. Keep in mind that you have a limited budget for building walls, and building walls on enemy's territory can cost 4x of your normal budget.

After building a wall, the land it wrapped around will be yours. However, you need exactly one round to strengthen your land, that is, if the land that you just claimed is partly or completely invaded by your opponent at the very next round, your whole territory claimed in that round will be revoked.

## How To Play

Left-click on any adjacent tile to start building. Left-click again to add nodes to your wall. After you have wrapped some lands, you may click the *Build* button to confirm your build this round. While building, right-click anywhere on the board or click the *Undo* button to undo your last operation. Click the *Skip* button to skip this round.
