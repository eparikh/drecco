# Rules
[Seesaw](https://cs.nyu.edu/courses/fall23/CSCI-GA.2965-001/seesaw.html), is a simple two-player board game where you aim to collect more weight on your side relative to your opponent. 

The board consists of a Seesaw. You and your opponent alternate turns placing Weights on your respective side of the board.The game is played twice, once as the first and once as the second player the player with the higher combined score wins.
