# Rules

Welcome to Superply Enhanced, a path building board game. The goal of player 1 is to build a path from left to right on the board grid. The goal of player 2 is to build a path from top to bottom. A path consists of a sequence of same-color squares such that each square in the path touches the next one on a side or on a corner.

To claim a square on a turn, the player must choose a grid position that satisfies the hint given by the computer. If the player's selection is not valid, no grid position will change color and the turn will go to the other player.

Superply Enhanced can be played using addition alone, subtraction alone, multiplication alone, or all mixed.
