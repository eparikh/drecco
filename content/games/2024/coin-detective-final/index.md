<h1 id="introduction">Introduction</h1>
<p>Someone is cheating at Coin Toss, and you have been sent to investigate! You are given
a set of coins, and your job is to figure out which way each one is biased before
your rival detective can do the same.</p>
<h1 id="rules">Rules</h1>
<ul>
<li>Each coin is biased with a higher probability toward heads or towards tails. The aim is to correctly guess the direction of the bias for all coins.</li>
<li>On each turn, a player can choose to either flip one coin, or enter a guess for all coins. If the player's guess is entirely correct, the player wins.</li>
<li>Each player gets a fixed number of guesses. When a player runs out of guesses, the other player wins by default.</li>
</ul>
<h1 id="tips">Tips</h1>
<ul>
<li>Parameters such as number of coins, number of guesses per player and biases of the coins may be configured at the game start.</li>
<li>Selecting "Show coin statistics" will display the flip history of each coin, making it easier to keep track of the flips.</li>
<li>You can use the Heads and Tails buttons to enter a guess, or use the H and T keys. You can also use the Backspace/Delete key to delete an entry.</li>
</ul>
