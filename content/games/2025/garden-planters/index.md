<h1 id="garden-planters">Garden Planters</h1>
<p>A strategic two-player board game where you move gardeners and plant flowers to outmaneuver your opponent. Play against an AI opponent or challenge a friend in this chess-meets-gardening strategy game!</p>
<h2 id="game-modes">Game Modes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>vs Computer</strong> - Play against a strategic AI opponent</li>
<li><strong>vs Player</strong> - Play with a friend on the same device (hot-seat mode)</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="difficulty-levels">Difficulty Levels</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Beginner</strong> - Sunflowers (15) and Rose Bushes (10) only - perfect for learning the basics</li>
<li><strong>Standard</strong> - All four flower types (Sunflowers, Roses, Lilies, Wildflowers) - the classic experience</li>
<li><strong>Advanced</strong> - All flowers plus special seasonal mechanics and garden features (Fountain, Compost Bin, Scarecrow)</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="advanced-level-features">Advanced Level Features</h2>
<p><strong>Four Seasons:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Spring (Turns 1-8):</strong> Normal gameplay</li>
<li><strong>Summer (Turns 9-16):</strong> Sunflowers block their square + 4 adjacent squares (up/down/left/right)</li>
<li><strong>Autumn (Turns 17-24):</strong> All flowers can be placed on FROM square OR any adjacent to destination</li>
<li><strong>Winter (Turn 25+):</strong> Gardeners can jump over Rose Bushes</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Garden Features (Once per game per player):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>⛲ Fountain:</strong> Teleport one gardener to any square within 2 spaces of center (5,5)</li>
<li><strong>🗑️ Compost Bin:</strong> Your next 2 flowers can be ANY type (even if depleted)</li>
<li><strong>🧹 Scarecrow:</strong> Opponent must place their next flower 3+ squares from all their gardeners</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-to-play">How to Play</h2>
<p><strong>Each turn has 3 simple steps:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>MOVE</strong> - Select your gardener and move it like a chess queen (any straight line)</li>
<li><strong>PLANT</strong> - Place a flower on the square you moved FROM</li>
<li><strong>END</strong> - Your turn ends automatically</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="flower-types">Flower Types</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>🌻 Sunflower</strong> (10) - Blocks its square</li>
<li><strong>🌹 Rose Bush</strong> (5) - Blocks its square + 4 diagonals (most powerful!)</li>
<li><strong>💧 Lily Pond</strong> (4) - Blocks its square</li>
<li><strong>🌈 Wildflower</strong> (5) - Place on FROM square OR any empty adjacent square</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="winning">Winning</h2>
<p>Force your opponent to run out of moves by strategically blocking the board with your flowers!</p>
<h2 id="scoring">Scoring</h2>
<p>After the game ends, earn points for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Each flower placed (1 point)</li>
<li>Connected groups of same flowers (+1 per flower bonus)</li>
<li>Rainbow Gardens: All 4 flower types in a 2×2 area (+10)</li>
<li>Winner bonus (+25)</li>
<li>Mobile gardeners at game end (+3 each)</li>
</ul>
<p>Outsmart the AI and create the most beautiful and strategic garden design!</p>
