<p>The year is 1967 and The Beatles have gone missing! Through meticulous research, we have uncovered that they're hiding in a yellow submarine somewhere in the vast ocean. Your mission is to navigate your probe through the waters to locate the elusive submarine.</p>
<p>Rules:</p>
<ul>
<li>Move your probe using arrow keys (Player 1) or WASD keys (Player 2).</li>
<li>Every few seconds, one of The Beatles will peek out to give you a hint about their location.</li>
<li>The probe scans in a radius that increases over time.</li>
<li>When you detect the submarine, you'll get a compass direction hint.</li>
<li>You can manually adjust the scan radius (use the slider) to narrow your search.</li>
<li>The submarine moves every few seconds to a new location, and The Beatles peek out again!</li>
<li>You win when you get within 2 units of the yellow submarine.</li>
</ul>
<p>Find The Beatles as fast as possible — your time is recorded on the local leaderboard!</p>
