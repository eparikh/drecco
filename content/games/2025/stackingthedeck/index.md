<p>You’re playing with <strong>16 cards</strong>: two copies of each number <strong>1 to 8</strong> (and <strong>1 means “ace”</strong>).</p>
<h3 id="the-two-players">The two players</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Chooser</strong>: tries to find a starting number that makes the jumps go <strong>past the end</strong> of the deck.</li>
<li><strong>Arranger</strong>: tries to set up the deck so that <strong>every</strong> starting number ends on the <strong>very last card</strong>, and that last card is a <strong>1</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="setup">Setup</h3>
<ol>
<li>The Chooser gets a bunch of <strong>white cards</strong> (numbers 1–8, with duplicates) and puts them in <strong>any order</strong> as a row.</li>
<li>The Arranger gets some <strong>blue “extra” cards</strong> (there must be <strong>at least one 1</strong>) and is allowed to <strong>slide them in between</strong> the white cards.
<ul>
<li>Important: the Arranger <strong>cannot change the order</strong> of the white cards — only insert blue cards between them.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Now the deck is finished and has <strong>16 cards</strong> total, left to right.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="playing-the-jumping-game">Playing the jumping game</h3>
<ol>
<li>The Chooser picks a starting number <strong>N</strong> between <strong>1 and 8</strong>.</li>
<li>Put a marker (a “dot”) on the <strong>Nth card from the left</strong> and look at its number. Call that number <strong>M</strong>.</li>
<li>Move the marker <strong>M cards to the right</strong> and look at the new card’s number.</li>
<li>Keep doing that: <strong>jump by the number you just landed on</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="who-wins">Who wins?</h3>
<ul>
<li>If a jump would make the marker go <strong>past the last card</strong>, the <strong>Chooser wins</strong>.</li>
<li>If the marker lands <strong>exactly on the last card</strong>, and that last card is a <strong>1</strong>, the <strong>Arranger wins</strong>.</li>
</ul>
