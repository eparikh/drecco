Game Info

How to play -

Air Drop is a two player strategy game. Each player takes turn dropping supplies to survivors seen within the drop's radius. The radius can be adjusted each turn, and all survivors within the radius will share a fixed amount of supplies equally. A player saves a survivor on the ground when he/she is the last person to fulfill the amount of supplies needed by the survivor.
