<p>
<b>How to Win</b>
<br>
The objective of the game is to save more survivors than your opponent. A player saves a survivor on the ground when he/she is the last person to fulfill the amount of supplies needed by the survivor.
</p>
<p>
<b>Turns</b>
<br>
Each player takes turn dropping supplies to survivors seen within the drop's radius
</p>
<p>
<b>Rules</b>
<br>
</p><ul>
<li>You can adjust the drop radius (in the range 10-50) on each turn</li>
<li>The number of supplies per drop is constant throughout the game, but can be set at the start of the game</li>
<li>The number of survivors on the ground is constant throughout the game, but can be set at the start of the game</li>
<li>All survivors within the raidus will share a fixed amount of supplies equally. The individual amount will be rounded down if it's not an integer</li>
</ul>
